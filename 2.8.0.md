# RoutePix v2.8.0

## What's New!
- **Parallel Cover Photo Loading**: Completely overhauled the home screen's network request engine. Cover photos for multiple trips now load concurrently, eliminating long black-screen wait times!
- **Smart "Solo / Duo / Group" Text Search**: You can now literally type "solo", "duo", or "group" into the search bar, and RoutePix will intelligently filter your timeline by Face Count (just like Google Photos), alongside the existing filter chips!
- **Polished UI Layouts**: Re-engineered the Timeline Top Bar to prevent overlapping buttons and ensure pristine spacing for trip titles and action icons (Story, Analytics, Info).
- **HD Story Quality Enforcement**: Story highlights now aggressively prioritize High-Definition original images (via `telegramDocumentId`) over compressed thumbnails, ensuring your memories look incredibly sharp on large screens.

## Notes for Upgrading
- **Legacy Photos & AI Face Detection**: Photos uploaded in older versions (prior to v2.8.0's Face Detection Engine) will have a default face count of `0` and will not appear under Solo/Duo/Group filters. 
- All newly uploaded photos will automatically be scanned via ML Kit and fully support Face Count filters!
