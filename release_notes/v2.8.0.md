# RoutePix v2.8.0 - The AI & Story Update 🚀

This is a massive update that completely rethinks how you view and interact with your shared memories. We've introduced Instagram-style Stories, on-device AI Face Detection, and a completely overhauled Home Screen. 

## ✨ Major Features

### 📖 Story Highlights
- **Instagram-Style Stories**: Relive your trip in a beautiful, immersive full-screen story experience.
- **Smart Curation Algorithm**: RoutePix automatically selects the best photos for your story, heavily prioritizing photos with people (using ML Kit Face Detection) and diverse scenery.
- **Intuitive Navigation**: Tap the right edge to advance, tap the left edge to go back, and watch the progress bar smoothly guide you through the highlights.
- **Uncompromised Quality**: Story highlights bypass the standard thumbnails and explicitly request the uncompressed, original High-Definition images (`telegramDocumentId`) directly from the Telegram CDN so they look stunning on large displays.

### 🕵️‍♂️ AI Face Detection & Smart Filters
- **On-Device Face Counting**: Every new photo uploaded is now processed locally by Google ML Kit to detect the number of faces (all completely offline and privacy-preserving).
- **Solo, Duo, & Group Filters**: Instantly filter your massive trip timeline using the new dedicated filter chips to find photos of yourself, couples, or large group shots.
- **Smart Text Search Integration**: You can now literally type the words "solo", "duo", or "group" into the search bar, and RoutePix will intelligently understand your intent and filter the timeline—just like Google Photos!

### 🏠 "My Trips" Home Screen Overhaul
- **Dynamic Cover Photos**: Gone are the static gray boxes! Trips now automatically generate beautiful cover art by fetching the best photos from the trip.
- **Concurrent HD Loading Architecture**: Re-engineered the underlying network engine. Cover photos for multiple trips now load concurrently in parallel, completely eliminating the long black-screen wait times of previous versions.
- **Glassmorphism Polish**: The home screen UI has been polished with modern translucency and cleaner typography.

### 🎨 Timeline UI Refinement
- **Re-engineered Top Bar**: The Timeline Top Bar has been completely restructured to prevent overlapping buttons on smaller screens. 
- **Dedicated Action Row**: Your trip name gets its own pristine space, with a dedicated, perfectly spaced row beneath it for all your actions (Story, Analytics, Search, Info, Sort).
- **Animated Search Bar**: The search bar and AI filter chips now live in a beautiful, space-saving animated collapsible menu.

## 📝 Notes for Upgrading
- **Legacy Photos**: Photos uploaded in older versions (prior to v2.8.0's Face Detection Engine) will have a default face count of `0` and will not appear under Solo/Duo/Group filters. 
- *Note for Admins*: We are actively developing a retroactive migration tool that will allow Trip Admins to seamlessly scan legacy photos and update their Face Counts without needing to re-upload any data!
- All newly uploaded photos will automatically be scanned via ML Kit and fully support Face Count filters!
